Pixel Legion: Gate Run Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy covers the Pixel Legion: Gate Run app for Android and iOS ("the app"), made by Chris Llaga ("we"). The free web version of the game shows no ads, sells nothing and sends nothing anywhere; this policy applies to it too.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting and no servers of its own. Your progress (level, coins, upgrades, sound settings) and whether you bought "Remove ads" are stored only on your device. We never see them. Deleting the app deletes them (your phone's own backup feature may keep a copy in your Google or Apple backup).

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows a short full-screen ad between some levels (never during play). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect and process data such as:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID on Android, and on iOS the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when asked;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and interactions with ads (views, taps);
  • diagnostic data about ad loading and app performance.

Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and explains how it uses data from partner apps here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent (Google's consent form) before any personalized ads; if you decline, you get non-personalized ads only, and you can change your choice at any time with the Ad privacy button in the menu. On iOS the app asks permission to track (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, the IDFA is not used. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Buying "Remove ads" turns ads off for good, and the ad software then no longer requests ads.

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. They process your payment and keep the purchase record under their own privacy policies; we receive no payment details, name or email. The app only asks the store whether this device's account owns "Remove ads" (including when you tap Restore purchases).

Children

The app is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you want any related data removed from Google's ad systems, contact us and we will help.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share any personal data, because we do not have any. The only data flows are the Google AdMob and app-store ones described above.
